Pegasus Rocket


The Pegasus is an air launched rocket developed by Orbital Science Corporation (OSC).This rocket is capable of carrying small payloads of upto 443kg into low earth orbit (LEO).This rocket first flew in 1990 and still remains as an important rocket and is still used today.The rocket uses 3 solid propellant stages and an optional monopropellant fourth stage.The first stage has a wing and a tail to provide lift and attitude control of the rocket.The main reason why this rocket is named pegasus as it has wings in the first stage and resembles of a horse with wings (pegasus).This rocket can also by released from an aircraft carrier at approximately 40,000ft.The pegasus's three Orion solid motors were developed by Hercules Aerospace specifically for the Pegasus Launcher.This was first launched in the United States.This rocket has a height of 16.9m with a diameter of 1.27m.The mass of this rocket is 18,500kg and this rocket has 3 stages
