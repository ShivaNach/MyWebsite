PSLV Rocket


PSLV or the Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle is an expendable medium lift launch vehicle designed by ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation).It was developed to allow India to launch its IRS (Indian Remote Sensing) satellites into Sun-synchronous orbits,a service that was,advent of the PSLV in 1993,commercially available only form Russia.The PSLV can also launch small size satellites into Geostationary Transfer Orbit (GTO).Some notable payloads launched but PSLV include India's first lunar probe Chandrayaan-1,India's first interplanetry mission,Mars orbiter mission (Mangalyaan)and India's first space observatory,Astrosat,World's first rocket to deploy 104 satellites-The PSLV C-37.Studies say that this vehicle is capable to deliver 600kg of payload to 550km from SHAR which began in 1978.This vehicle has 2 strap-on boosters and has a height of 44m,diameter of 2.8m which mass of average 282,000kg and has 4 stages.
