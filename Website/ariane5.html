Ariane 5 Rocket


Ariane 5 is an European heavy-lift launch vehicle that is part of the Ariane rocket family.This is also an expendable launch system designed by the French government space agency.It is used to deliver payloads into GTO orbit or LEO orbit.The Ariane project was instrumental in helping to overcome the European space crisis.Ariane 5 rockets are manufactured under the authority of ESA and the French space agency.Ariane 5 succeeded Ariane 4 but was not derived form it.Unlike all the ariane rockets,this rocket was a new model without any modifications of the other Arianes.Ariane 5's brogenic H173 main stage is called the EPC.It consists of a large tank 30.5m high with two compartments,one for liquid oxygen and the other for the liquid hydrogen.This rocket has a height of 46-52m with a diameter of 5.4m.This rocket has a mass of 777,000kg and has 2 stages.The payload to LEO is around 16,000kg and payload to GTO is 6,950kg.
