What is a Rocket?

A Rocket is a Launch Vehicle which can obtain thrust to go in outer space.Rocket engine if formed entirely form propellant caried within the rocket before use.A rocket is a vehicle which has the ablity to take probes,rovers satellites and even humans to space.It generally takes satellites to the orbits of the earth such as LEO (Low Earth Orbit),GTO (Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit) and GEO (Geostationary Equatorial Orbit). These are the main orbits where a satellite moves around the earth.Rockets are huge masses which have many stages.The stages of the rocket breaks down as it reaches a particular point.An average rocket has a height of a 15 story building! Ultimately,only the top capsule of the rocket ejects the satellites into space.Most of the rocket has some strap boosters which gives an additional thurst to the rocket.There are located in the side to the rocket and breaks down during the first stage.Each rocket made by different space agencies have different builds.The main part of the rocket is the fuel tank,capsule,and the engine are the three main parts of a rocket.As the stages breaks up,the next stage's engine gets activated and starts up the rocket.
