Ariane 3 Rocket


Ariane 3 was a European expendable carrier rocket.This rocket was used for 11 launches between 1984 and 1989.This rocket belongs to the Ariane family and was derived form the Ariane 2 rocket.The Ariane 3 followed the same basic design as the Airane 1 but has some modifications.This rocket has a two solid-fuelled PAP strap-on-booster which was used to augment the first stage on life-off.The core of this rocket is based on its earlier version ariane 2 and is just made from the upgrades of ariane 2.The first stage was powered by four viking 2B bipropellant engines,this used a dinitrogen teroxide oxideser.The second stage was powered by the viking 4B which used the same fuel-oxidiser.The third stage was powered by the cryogenic fuelled HM7B engine.On some of the rockets,additional fourth stage was added and was powred by the Mage 2 engine.This rocket has a height of 49.13m with a diameter of 3.8m.This rocket has a mass of 234,000kg.The payload to GTO is 2,700kg.
