SLV Rocket


The SLV or the Satellite Launch Vehicle was a project started in the early 1970s by ISRO(Indian Space Research Organisation) to develop the technology needed for launching the rocket.SLV was intended to reach a height of 400 kilometers and carry a payload of 40kg.This SLV rocket was a four-stage rocket with all solid-propellant motors.The first launch took place in Sriharikota on 10th August 1979.The solid motor case for first and second stage are fabricated form 15 CDV6 steel sheets and third and fourth stages from fiber reinforced plastic.This rocket has a height of 22m,diameter of 1m,mass of 17,000kg.
