Falcon Heavy



Falcon Heavy Rocket



Falcon Heavy is a large and a partially reusable heavy-lift launch vehicle designed and manufactured by SpaceX.This Rocket has a strengthened Falcon 9-like first stage.It has a two additional first stages as strap-on boosters.The Falcon heavy has one of the highest payload capacity of any currently operational launch vehicle.Initially,this rocket was mainly designed to carry humans into space beyond low Earth orbit.Later,it was not given a human-rating certification for carrying humans to space.The falcon heavy was designed from the falcon 9's fuselage(body of the rocket).This rocket has a height of 70m with a diameter of 3.66m.It has a width is 12.2m and has a mass of 1,420,000kg.It has 2 stages and can also have additional stages if required.It's payload to LEO is aorund 64,000kg and has a payload of 27,000kg to GTO.
