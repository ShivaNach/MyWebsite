Dragon 2 Spacecraft


Dragon 2 or Crew Dragon is a reusable spacecraft developed and manufactured by SpaceX.The space launches atop a Flacon 9 Block 5 rocket and returns via ocean splash down.The Dragon has a large windows and new flight computers and solar arrays than the Dragon.There are two variations of this spacecraft,one for a crew and one for the cargo of the parts,satellites,etc.The crew Dragon has an integrated escape system which allows the astronaut to escape in an emergency.The designed life of dragon is 1 week(free flight) and 210 days when docked to ISS.It has a dry mass of 10,000kg and a payload of 6,000kg.7 people can sit in this machine.It has a diameter of 4m and height of 8.1m with a side wall angle of 15 degrees.
