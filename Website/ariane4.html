Ariane 4 Rocket


The Ariane 4 is an expendable launch system.This is a first flight on 15th June 1988 until the final flight,which was preformed on 15th February 2003.This rocket has attained 113 successful launches out of 116 conducted.In 1982,the Ariane 4 programme was approved by the European Space Agency (ESA).The Ariane 4 was the ultimate development from the preceding members of the Ariane family.Compared with the Ariane 2 and Ariane 3,the Ariane 4 featured a streached first and third stages.The rocket was used in a number of variants - it colud be fitted with two or four additional solid and a liquid fuelled booster rockets.The engine used in this rocket is Viking 6.this rocket can use either of 0,2 and 4 no. of boosters.This rocket has a height of 58.72m with a mass of 3.8m.This rocket has a mass of 240,000kg.The payload to LEO of this rocket is 5,000kg and 2,000kg to GTO orbit.
