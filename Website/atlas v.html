Atlas V Rocket


Atlas V is the fifth major version in the Atlas Rocket family.This expendable launch system was originally engineered by Lockheed Martin and is now operated by United Launch Alliance (ULA).This rocket consists of 2 main stages.The first stage is powred by Russian RD-180 engine and runs with the help of RD Amross and burning kerosene and liquid oxygen.The second stage is made up of the RL 10 engine made by US and uses liquid oxygen and liquid hydrogen to propel.This rocket is said to have AJ-60A strap-on boosters.This rocket helped in the transportation of cargo to space stations.This rocket has a height of 58.3m with a diameter of 3.81m and has a mass of 590,000kg.The payload to LEO is around 8,250kg and has a payload of around 4,750kg to GTO.
