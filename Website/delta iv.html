Delta IV Rocket


Delta IV is an expendable launch system in the Delta rocket family.The main components were designed by Boeing's Defense and Security division.Delta IV vehicles are currently built in the United Launch Alliance (ULA).This rocket can launch commercial satellite payloads and also heavy payloads.One of the main purpose of this rocket is to launch U.S. military Payloads.This rocket uses liquid hydrogen to propel for launch.The minimum height of this rocket is 63m to a maximum of 72m.It has a diameter of 5m and has a mass of approx. 250,000kg.The payload to LEO for this rocket is 11,470kg and has a payload of 4,440kg to GTO.
